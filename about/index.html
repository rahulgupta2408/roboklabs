About Robok Labs

End-to-end electronics product design and manufacturing, specializing in industrial automation and intelligent embedded systems.

WHO WE ARE

Built to engineer complete products.

Robok Labs is a Toronto-based electronics product design and manufacturing company. We take products from idea to production-ready reality — handling every engineering discipline so you don't have to stitch together multiple vendors.

Our core domain is industrial automation: ruggedized control electronics, embedded sensing platforms, and digital twin software that gives plant operators real-time visibility into their equipment.

What We Build

  • Electronics hardware (analog, digital, power systems)
  • Embedded firmware — bare-metal, RTOS, and connected
  • Industrial automation and control systems
  • Digital twin platforms and SCADA integration
  • Mechanical enclosures and IP-rated industrial packaging
  • PCB assembly and full box build integration
  • DFM/DFT — designed for real manufacturing from day one

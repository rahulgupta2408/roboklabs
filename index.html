WHAT WE DO

One partner.
The full lifecycle.

Robok Labs is a Toronto-based electronics product design and manufacturing company. We take products from an idea to a production-ready reality — handling every step so you don't have to stitch together multiple vendors.

Our core domain is industrial automation: ruggedized control electronics, embedded sensing platforms, and industrial-grade digital twins that give plant operators real-time visibility into their machines.

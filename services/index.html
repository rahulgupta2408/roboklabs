ENGINEERING SERVICES

Design Services

Full-spectrum electronics product design — from hardware schematic to shipping firmware to production-ready software, all under one roof.

WHAT WE DO

Our Design Services

Every capability needed to take an electronics product from concept to validated, production-ready design.

⚡

Electronics Hardware Design

Schematic capture, multi-layer PCB layout, component selection, signal integrity, power management, and production-ready design with strong DFM focus. Altium Designer and KiCad workflows.

💾

Firmware Development

From bare-metal to RTOS-based embedded solutions. Industrial protocols (CAN, Modbus, OPC-UA, MQTT), motor control, sensor fusion, and secure OTA update pipelines for ARM Cortex-M platforms.

🔲

FPGA Design

High-performance digital logic development including VHDL/Verilog design, timing closure, IP integration, and hardware acceleration workflows for Xilinx and Intel platforms.

🖥️

Software & Dashboard Development

Backend systems, APIs, SCADA integration, digital twin dashboards, and cloud-connected platforms that extend the value of your hardware and put real-time data in front of operators.

📱

App Development

Responsive mobile and web applications that connect to your devices for control, remote monitoring, data visualization, configuration, and field diagnostics.

🔩

Mechanical Design

CAD modelling, structural design, DIN rail packaging, IP-rated enclosures, thermal management, and tolerance analysis for industrial deployment environments. SolidWorks and Fusion 360.

🎛️

UI/UX Design

Clean, operator-focused interfaces for HMI panels, industrial dashboards, mobile apps, and embedded displays. Designed for real-world use in demanding environments.

🔬

Prototyping

Rapid prototyping to validate design assumptions early — proof-of-concept hardware, functional assemblies, 3D-printed enclosures, and bring-up support before committing to production.

🧪

Testing Support

Functional testing, integration testing, reliability assessment, environmental stress testing (temperature, vibration, humidity), and engineering validation across the development cycle.

✅

Qualification & Certification Support

We design with certification in mind from day one — supporting documentation, EMC pre-compliance, and engineering coordination for CE, FCC, CSA, UL, IEC, and other relevant standards.

HOW WE WORK

Our Design Process

A structured process built around clarity, technical accountability, and shipping products that work in the real world.

01

Concept & Requirements

We start by deeply understanding your product goals, application environment, technical constraints, and business requirements to define the right system architecture.

02

Design & Development

Our team executes hardware design, firmware development, prototyping, and engineering reviews — moving from architecture to a manufacturable, validated design iteratively.

03

Validation & Handoff

We support functional testing, manufacturing readiness, DFM review, and technical handoff — so your product moves forward with reduced risk and full documentation.

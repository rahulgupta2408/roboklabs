PCB ASSEMBLY

PCB Assembly

From prototype to high-volume production. Our PCB assembly services cover SMT, through-hole, and mixed-technology assemblies for a wide range of board complexities and order volumes.

We manage the complete build — BOM sourcing, stencil printing, placement, reflow, inspection, and functional test — so you receive boards that are ready to deploy.

  • SMT & through-hole assembly, including BGA and fine-pitch
  • Prototype to production volume scaling
  • Lead-free & RoHS compliant processes
  • Full BOM management & component sourcing
  • Automated optical inspection (AOI)
  • In-circuit testing (ICT) & functional test
  • Conformal coating and underfill options

BOX BUILD ASSEMBLY

Box Build Assembly

Complete system-level integration for your finished product. We take your PCBAs, enclosures, cables, and components and build them into a tested, shippable product.

Box build is where hardware design meets the real world. We manage the integration, harness assembly, labelling, and final functional verification so your product arrives ready.

  • Full system integration: PCBs, PSUs, connectors, displays
  • Custom cable and wiring harness assembly
  • Enclosure population and hardware installation
  • DIN rail and panel mount system builds
  • Labelling, firmware flashing, and configuration
  • Final functional test and quality sign-off
  • Packaging and logistics preparation

QUALITY & CAPABILITIES

Built for industrial standards.

Our manufacturing processes are designed to meet the reliability and traceability requirements of industrial, medical, and embedded electronics applications.

🔍

100% AOI Inspection

Every assembled board goes through automated optical inspection to catch placement and solder defects before they reach you.

🌿

RoHS Compliant

Lead-free assembly process using compliant solder alloys and materials, meeting EU RoHS and REACH directives.

📋

Full Traceability

Component lot tracking, assembly records, and test data retained per build for full traceability and field serviceability.

Functional Testing

In-circuit and functional test support for every build — not just visual inspection. We verify your board works before it ships.

IPC Standards

Prototype to Production
